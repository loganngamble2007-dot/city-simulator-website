City Simulator

An incremental city-builder. Start with six cavemen in the Stone Age and grow a small settlement into a thriving city in the sky.

From six cavemen to a city in the sky

Your story begins with just six cavemen. Collect wood, food, water, and stone, keep your people fed and sheltered, and help your settlement grow — one decision, one building, and one discovery at a time.

Villagers choose their own jobs, so every settlement develops a little differently. Watch woodcutters, farmers, hunters, builders, researchers, warriors, and more find their place as your city takes shape.

Features

🏕️ Build and upgrade homes

Build tents that upgrade to huts, then houses, and eventually sky lofts.

🧑‍🌾 Villagers choose their jobs

Woodcutters, farmers, hunters, builders, researchers, warriors, and more — your villagers pick the work that fits them.

📈 Job skill levels 1–5

As villagers work, they gain experience and improve their job skills from level 1 to level 5.

🗺️ Five eras to research

Research your way through five eras, from the Stone Age all the way to the Sky Age.

🛡️ Survive the raids

Prepare your people and your defenses — a raid arrives every 10 years.

🧭 A 49-quest guided chain

Follow a guided chain of 49 quests as you learn the game and lead your city forward.

🏥 A modern-era city

Reach the modern era and add buildings such as a church, a hospital, and fire and police stations.

🌲 Gather and grow

Collect wood, food, water, and stone, and balance what your growing city needs next.
